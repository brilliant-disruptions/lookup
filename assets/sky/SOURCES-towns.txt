assets/sky/towns.json.gz — every town and city of 5,000+ people, used only
for place search, which runs on the phone (domain/places/town_search.dart).

Source: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/ — licensed under Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Files from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/: cities5000.zip,
admin1CodesASCII.txt, countryInfo.txt (fetched 2026-11-27).

Changed: built with tool/places/build.py, which keeps only the name, the
ASCII name, latitude and longitude rounded to 2 places, population, and the
region and country names. Rows are [name, ascii or "", lat, lon,
population, region, country], largest first.
