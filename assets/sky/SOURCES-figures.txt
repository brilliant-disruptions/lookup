assets/sky/figures/*.png and assets/sky/figures.json: the constellation
artwork drawn faintly behind the lines in the Sky tab's panorama, pinned to
the sky by three anchor stars each. Rebuilt with tool/figures/build.py.

Baroque artwork (23 figures: Andromeda, Auriga, Bootes, Cancer, Canes
Venatici, Canis Major, Capricornus, Cetus, Columba, Coma Berenices, Corona
Borealis, Cygnus, Draco, Gemini, Grus, Hercules, Leo, Lepus, Orion, Perseus,
Piscis Austrinus, Scorpius, Ursa Major): Johannes Hevelius, Firmamentum
Sobiescianum sive Uranographia (1690), public domain, scans via Wikimedia
Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Uranographia_(Hevelius)).
Cleaned into light linework (mirrored to sky view, plate lines, lettering,
engraved stars and neighbouring figures removed) and re-anchored with
tool/figures/hevelius/ (figs.json records each plate's Commons file name).
No Bode (Uranographia, 1801) plates are used yet.

Every other figure: Stellarium's "modern" sky culture illustrations
(skycultures/modern/illustrations/, https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium),
by the Stellarium project, used under the Free Art License 1.3
(https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/). Copied unchanged.
The Hevelius figures' anchors are fitted to the plates, not taken from
Stellarium.

Anchor data: Stellarium skycultures/modern/index.json (image sizes, anchor
pixel positions and Hipparcos numbers), CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). figures.json is adapted
from it (Hipparcos numbers replaced by J2000 positions) and is shared under
the same licence.

Anchor star positions: d3-celestial data/stars.6.json (BSD-3, see
LICENSE-d3-celestial.txt); HIP 17999, 91589 and 102805, fainter than its
magnitude-6 cut, from the HYG database v4.0 (https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database),
CC BY-SA 4.0.

Serpens has no picture; Puppis and Vela are part of Carina's Argo Navis.

Reference redraws: the pictures for And, Aur, Boo, Cas, CMa, Cnc, Cep, Cet,
Col, Com, CrB, CVn, Cyg, Dra, Gem, Gru, Her, Leo, Lep, Lib, Ori, Per, PsA,
Sco, UMa and Vul are redrawn Hevelius linework from a user-supplied
reference set ("interior-png"). Their source and licence are not recorded
here and must be confirmed before release. Each was registered onto the
cleaned Hevelius figure, so it keeps that figure's three anchor stars.
