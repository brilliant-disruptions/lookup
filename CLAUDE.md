# LookUp — notes for agents

- **Keep `lib/domain/` pure Dart.** No Flutter, no plugins, no `dart:io`. Everything there must be unit-testable with `flutter test` and no device.
- **Alerts are the product.** Any change to `AlertPlanner`, `PassPredictor` thresholds, cloud gating or quiet hours needs a test in `test/domain/`. A false alarm is the one thing this app must never do.
- **What has gone out stays out of the schedule** (`domain/scheduling/alert_ledger.dart`, kept by `RefreshService`): a refresh cancels only alerts still waiting, never one already delivered (it's on their Lock Screen), never sends one twice ("aurora now" once per 3-hour bucket), and counts tonight's delivered taps against `maxAlertsPerNight` (`AlertPlanner.plan(alerted:)`). A heads-up that would land in quiet hours waits for them to end. **Nothing is said from stale data:** no taps from orbital elements older than `AlertPlanner.stationElementsMaxAge` / `trainElementsMaxAge`, a Starlink train is only a train while its revolution count (carried on from the elements' date) says so, "aurora now" needs a reading under an hour old taken for this place (`AuroraSnapshot.currentFor`) and the Sun 12° down (`auroraDarkDeg`), and a forecast for another town is dropped, not reused. Aurora wording faces the nearer pole (`auroraFace`). Tests: `test/domain/alert_ledger_test.dart`, `test/services/delivered_alerts_test.dart`.
- **Never show azimuth/elevation numbers on the main path.** Use `domain/sky/direction.dart` words. Numbers live behind the Look sheet's detail line, or beside the words when **Nerd mode** is on (Settings, `LookUpSettings.nerdMode`, off by default; formatters `nerd*` in `ui/format.dart`). A test pins no degree sign in the camera view with it off.
- **Data etiquette.** Celestrak: at most once a day per feed. SWPC: ≥ 30 min. Open-Meteo: ≥ 6 h and non-commercial until billing ships. Relay rare events (`/v1/rare-events`): at most once a day, even on pull-to-refresh.
- **Fixtures** in `test/fixtures/` are real Celestrak records from 2026-10-20; tests pin times near that epoch. Don't "fix" a test by widening tolerances — check the sky first (ISS visibility comes in ~2-week cycles; some weeks genuinely have none).
- Run before pushing: `flutter analyze && flutter test`.
- Spec: `brilliant-disruptions/app-ideas/look_up_now/spec.md`.
- **iOS native code** (`ios/Runner/AppDelegate.swift`, `ios/LookUpWidgets/`) can't be compiled on Linux; at least run `swiftc -parse` on edited files. The two `LookUpActivityAttributes` structs must stay identical.
- **No backend, no third-party SDKs.** Everything runs on the phone.
- **Only a rough place leaves the phone** (`roughCoord` in `domain/privacy/rough_place.dart`: two decimals, about a kilometre): to Open-Meteo for the forecast, and stamped on saved pictures and timelapses. The sky maths keeps the real fix (eclipse and transit margins need it). `Info.plist`'s location strings, `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`, the privacy page (`relay/src/pages.ts`) and the App Privacy answers (`docs/mac-checklist.md`) all say so: change them together.
- **The fixed sky** (`lib/domain/sky/catalog.dart`, `assets/sky/`) is J2000 data from d3-celestial (BSD-3; keep `assets/sky/LICENSE-d3-celestial.txt`). Planet maths is Schlyter's; tests pin oppositions and elongations to published dates, so don't loosen them.
- **"Can I see it?"** lives in `domain/sky/visibility.dart`. It must never say "Easy to see" through cloud, rain, fog or a washed-out sky: weather and moonlight checks come before the positive verdicts.
- **Pause all alerts** (`LookUpSettings.alertsPaused`, Settings › Alerts) plans nothing at all without touching what they chose to hear about. Settings also says when notifications are off for LookUp at system level. **Android schedules to the minute where it's allowed** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `NotificationService.exactTimingAllowed`; Settings › Alerts › On-time alerts asks) and falls back to ordinary timing; release signing reads `android/key.properties` (`docs/android-release.md`), else the debug key, for testing only. The week-ahead note only calls a week "quiet" from the day before it starts, when the week's passes can be seen.
- **Not everything is pushed.** `SkyEventType.pushes` is false for the aurora outlook, ISS transits and satellites-tonight; `AlertPlanner` must never schedule those (test pins it). Transits last under a second and are for planning.
- **Native bits waiting on a Mac** are listed in `docs/mac-checklist.md` (time-sensitive capability, app group, widget extension, `ios/Runner/LookUpIntents.swift` not yet in the Runner target). Don't add uncompiled Swift to a target from Linux: it breaks Xcode Cloud.
- **Notification sound:** Android fixes a channel's sound at creation, so changing `lookup_chime` means a new channel id in `NotificationService`.
- **iOS plugins resolve through Swift Package Manager.** Xcode Cloud disables automatic package resolution, so a plugin that pulls a *remote* Swift package (e.g. sentry_flutter → sentry-cocoa) breaks the build unless `ios/Runner.xcodeproj/project.xcworkspace/xcshareddata/swiftpm/Package.resolved` is generated on a Mac and committed. Check new iOS plugins' `Package.swift` for `url:` dependencies before adding them.
- **Sky glow map** (`assets/sky/skyglow.bin.gz`, `domain/sky/skyglow.dart`) is an estimate from NASA Black Marble + Natural Earth, rebuilt with `tool/skyglow/`. Keep `assets/sky/SOURCES-skyglow.txt`, never suggest a spot over water, and don't loosen `darkSkySqm`/`citySkySqm` (see `tool/skyglow/README.md`).
- **Sky through history** (3000 BC – 3000 AD) is **hidden in the app for now** (`skyThroughHistory` in `ui/sky/sky_screen.dart`: no "Any night" or famous nights, and Explore's Day steps stop a week either way with no LookUp+ ask). Keep the maths and its pins so it can come back: `domain/sky/astro.dart` adds ΔT (`delta_t.dart`), quadratic precession, the IAU obliquity and IAU sidereal time to Schlyter, and the Moon is Meeus ch. 47 (secular terms included); stars are precessed from J2000 with Hipparcos proper motions (`assets/sky/proper_motion.json`, keep `SOURCES-proper-motion.txt`). `test/domain/history_test.dart` pins the Sun, Moon and planets to JPL Horizons and every famous night (`domain/sky/history.dart`) to what Horizons shows; don't loosen those, and check any new famous night against Horizons first. Dates before 15 Oct 1582 are Julian (`calendar.dart`); before 1900 times are local mean time.
- **The Moon is occultation grade**: Meeus ch. 47 with exact topocentric parallax, within 12″ of Horizons from the ground (`docs/moon-accuracy.md`, `test/domain/moon_accuracy_test.dart`). Don't loosen that pin or go back to a shorter series; occultation alerts depend on it.
- **"You weren't alone"** (`domain/satellites/under_the_pass.dart`, `assets/sky/places.json` from Natural Earth via `tool/population/`; keep `assets/sky/SOURCES-places.txt`) counts people *under* a pass in a dark sky, never people who *saw* it: clouds aren't counted and the card says so. Don't reword it into a claim about sightings.
- **Solar eclipses** (`domain/events/solar_eclipses.dart`) are computed from NASA's Besselian elements (`besselian_elements.dart`, copied unchanged from the GSFC search engine; add new eclipses the same way, keep 2017 and 2024). Tests pin Dallas/Carbondale/Luxor/Nashville to published local circumstances; don't loosen them. "Total" is only said with a ~3 km margin inside the path (else "near the path"), and every eclipse alert carries the eclipse-glasses line. They're free on purpose.
- **Constellation pictures** (`assets/sky/figures/`, `domain/sky/figures.dart`, `ui/sky/figure_art.dart`) are mostly redrawn Hevelius linework from NASA's Universe of Learning (public domain; NASA and NASA's UoL ask for credit, see SOURCES; `hand_*.png` are hand-anchored), plus cleaned Hevelius plates (1690, public domain via Wikimedia Commons; rebuilt with `tool/figures/hevelius/clean.py` from `figs.json`) and Stellarium's modern illustrations for the rest (Free Art License; anchors CC BY-SA 4.0; `tool/figures/build.py`). Keep `assets/sky/SOURCES-figures.txt`. They stay faint and only the centred one brightens: they explain the lines, never hide stars.
- **Occultations** (`domain/events/occultations.dart`) only say "covers" when the track passes the margin inside the Moon's edge (stars 1′, planets 3′ plus disk); closer is "near the Moon" with no times. Budgets are in `docs/moon-accuracy.md`; `test/domain/occultations_test.dart` pins IOTA times and the Taiwan limit. Don't shrink the margins or loosen those.
- **LookUp+ is the store's to give and take** (`services/plus_service.dart`, `domain/plus/plus_grant.dart`). `isPlus` is saved with its end date and re-read from the store at every launch and in the background refresh; never set it by hand or go back to a flag that stays on. On iPhone the question is the plugin's `restorePurchases()` (StoreKit 2 current entitlements at in_app_purchase_storekit 0.4.15: silent, answered on the purchase stream); re-read the plugin before bumping it. An empty answer is doubted for `PlusGrant.doubt`, counted from the first empty answer while the paid period is still running (`plusDoubtAt`; never from the last vouch, which may be old), and the background never takes LookUp+ away on one; a Play purchase that is listed but unpaid (pending) isn't LookUp+: a paying subscriber losing alerts is as bad as a false one. Tests in `test/domain/plus_grant_test.dart` and `test/services/plus_service_test.dart`.
- **Nothing anyone can post to the relay is trusted.** Aurora reports can't raise an alert the model wouldn't (`crowdConfirms` runs `auroraNow` first; test pins it) and a wake-up is an unforced refresh. Invite words pass `linkish()` in `relay/src/invite.ts` (no web address, domain shape, IP or phone number; checked again when served); `test/services/invite_client_test.dart` keeps a copy of those rules, so change both together.
- **The Look sheet's way in is Pip** (`_PipShowsYou`: a card with Pip awake, not a plain button), with Photograph it / Calendar / Invite as one row of tiles; both use `kit/pressable.dart` so they sink under the finger. Keep keys `pip-show-you`, `photograph-it`, `add-to-calendar`, `invite` (`test/ui/look_sheet_actions_test.dart`). **Taking a picture is never shown as locked, and never costs a tap:** Photograph it carries no LookUp+ tag; on free it opens Pip's camera straight away (no choice sheet), and the object sheet's paid button says "Pro camera · LookUp+". **Pro is mentioned without pestering:** one dismissible line on the caught-it screen (`_ProTip`), by the rule in `domain/plus/pro_nudge.dart`: never on the first catch, never for a pass, at most once a week, never after two "Not now"s, and only when no Plus-only sight is offered there. Never a pop-up (tests in `test/domain/pro_nudge_test.dart`, `test/ui/pro_tip_test.dart`).
- **LookUp+ is offered by one budget** (`docs/plus-strategy.md`). Soft asks (Home's one card: storm, "You missed it", near you; after a catch: a sight coming or the Pro line) go through `domain/plus/nudges.dart`: one per session, none in the first two days except a storm, none after two declines, never on LookUp+, never a pop-up. Use `softAskAllowed` / `claimSoftAsk` / `openSoftPaywall` / `declineSoftAsk` (`ui/soft_asks.dart`) for any new one. "You missed it" only for a real past sight in a clear sky (unknown cloud never counts). A kind they switched off is never pitched (`AlertPlanner.typeEnabled`; no storm card with aurora off), and the onboarding offer never says "you asked" for a switch left at its default. The onboarding offer shows once. Limits someone reaches for (a later night, a second place, the photo caption) open the paywall directly. Tests: `test/domain/plus_asks_test.dart`, `test/ui/home_ask_test.dart`.
- **Built for a small phone held upright** (375×667 pt is the owner's): the app is portrait only (`main.dart`); the Week tab shows the whole week at once (`ui/week_views.dart`: one bar per day, the hours with nothing on cut out as a zig-zag break (`DayAxis`) and the rest fitted to the width, so nothing is swiped sideways; each sight on its real time (`layoutBar`), and only a day whose sights pile up grows a second row; one-line day labels and a two-line pick card; Last week and Today and ahead are tabs; `test/ui/week_view_test.dart` pins a week in view at 375×667 with no sideways scroll); a row's time is never the part that's cut off (`fmtDayShortTime`); onboarding's Next stays pinned (`_PinnedButton`). The screen stays on while Pip guides (`services/keep_awake.dart`). Catch it's picture takes less of a short screen so its words and buttons fit (`_pictureShare`); `LOOKUP_SMALL=1` renders the Catch it screenshots at 375×667 to check. Posters are shared pictures and ignore the phone's text size; the header's place chip becomes a pin when there's no room for a name; pages of settings build every row so they can open scrolled to a section. Times follow the phone's clock (`use24HourClock`), and noon to five is "afternoon", not "tonight". **Nothing is lost or silent:** leaving Catch it with a finished picture not yet in the Log asks first (`keep-before-leaving`); a refused camera offers Open Settings (`showCameraTrouble`); the paywall says when a purchase didn't go through, what Restore found, and where a subscriber manages or cancels; a screen that fails to load says so kindly with Try again (`kit/load_trouble.dart`), never the raw error.
- **Tabs are Home, Week, Explore, Log, with Pip beside them** (`HomeTab` in `state/providers.dart`; `ui/pip/pip_button.dart`): a tap on Pip opens the live sky camera from anywhere. Week is the every-night list (`upcoming_screen.dart`); Home's "See every night" switches to it. Home says the next thing fast (one sentence, one clouds pill, one button, three rows); it's for kids and adults, so keep it that short.
- **Pip is a green dot with eyes and water in it.** One character, no style setting. Its hello (`PipState.greet`, `pipGreeting` in `kit/pip_motion.dart`, painted by `kit/pip_greeting.dart`): it wobbles like water and sends out a ripple, opens into a soft orb with two small eyes, glances aside, lifts a droplet off its top and watches it fall back in with a splash, blinks, and closes back into the dot. Eyes only: never a mouth, hands or outline; it's a light with a little life in it, not a cartoon. **At rest Pip is the character** (`pipCharacter`: breathes, glances, blinks, jiggles now and then, squishes when touched via `PipMark.poke`); it only becomes the plain dot to guide in the camera (`PipAim` morphs it down on launch and back up on the way home) or to mark a spot in a sky picture (`PipMark(asDot: true)`). Reduce Motion shows the orb and eyes still. **Pip's feelings are all in its eyes** (`PipMood`, `PipEyes` in `kit/pip_motion.dart`; painted by `_paintEye`): calm, happy (smiling eyes), amazed (wide, a glint), curious (tilt, one eye wider), sleepy (heavy lids, slow blinks), sorry (outer corners droop), focused (lids slant in), wink, shy. Lid shape, size, a glint and where it looks: never a mouth, brows, tears or hearts. A new feeling eases in (`pipMoodChange`); `PipMark(mood:, moodFor:)`. Where: the caught-it Pip (`caughtMood`: wink for a secret, amazed for a first catch or gold, else happy), the Look sheet's card (sorry under cloud, amazed when live), the Pip beside the tabs (sleepy by day and in quiet hours, `domain/pip/pip_rest.dart`), onboarding (curious hello, focused while finding their sky, shy at the alert ask), the Photos sheet (shy), and "can't see" (dim) is sorry. Pip never looks happy about a sky you can't see, and never stays sad. Tests: `test/ui/pip_moods_test.dart`. Nothing on a camera screen points a direction but Pip: no crosshairs or ticks (`reticle.dart` is a plain ring, and the sky camera has none).
- **In the sky camera Pip guides; it doesn't sit at the bottom.** It sets off from its button beside the tabs (`PipAim.launchFrom` = `pipHomeOffset`) and on Close flies back down into it before the camera fades (`pipCameHome` makes the button's Pip land); it flies to what it's leading you to (`domain/finder/guide_target.dart`: a pass going over now, else the brightest planet, else the Moon, then the rest; nothing under 5°), waits at the edge on the side to turn, and once you've brought it within 10° it lands on it with a small burst and stays the plain dot: no face or wave on a target. Tap Pip there to catch it. "Pip, find something else" steps through the same order.
- **A catch is never dropped from the Log**: only "missed it" answers are capped (`FeedbackStore.trimLog`); catching something again keeps the day it was first caught; patches go by when it was caught (`Sighting.at`), not the event's own time.
- **Only a catch logs a sighting.** In the camera, with something in the middle Pip sits on it, and a tap on Pip (or the shutter) takes the picture, saves it to Photos and to the Log (`keepCatch` in `ui/keep_catch.dart`, which every camera path uses). Catch it (`catch_it_screen.dart`) is the other camera path. No button, alert action or Lock Screen tap logs on its own (tests in `test/services/alert_actions_test.dart`, `lock_screen_catches_test.dart`); "No, I missed it" answers still go in, for tuning.
- **Onboarding is Follow Pip** (`ui/onboarding_screen.dart`, `ui/onboarding/`, rules in `domain/onboarding/first_look.dart`): tap the light and Pip wakes; Pip asks for a rough location (or a town; allowed but no fix also goes to the town picker); during the quick refresh Pip lights up their sky (`LightUpSky`: the real stars over their place fly out from Pip to where they sit, the horizon rises with the place's name, the lines draw in; its last frame is the sky page's first); then their real sky (`FirstLookSky`: the camera's `SkyOverlayPainter` and `PipAim`, turned by dragging), right now if it's dark, else tonight a little after dark (never a time that's gone); Pip leads to the brightest thing up and a tap catches it; then the next space station pass sped up (a practice pass if none in three days); the day slider (`stationSight`: why passes come at dusk and dawn); "What should Pip tell you about?" (the alert settings themselves, with no prices on it); the alert ask; the one-time offer, "Light up your sky" (`ui/onboarding/offer_sky.dart`): it says LookUp+ once and builds on what they left on ("Aurora and Starlink trains are LookUp+"), then their sky dimmed with a switch per LookUp+ thing (aurora and Starlink only if left on, darker skies, timelapses, Pro camera) that turns it on in the sky while Pip flies to it; aurora and Starlink also drop in the tap they'd get (the real coming one's words, else marked "Example"); flipping them never changes settings. Then the real sights coming and the rest of LookUp+ from `ui/plus_perks.dart` (the paywall's list too), with its buttons pinned so they never scroll away. The sky through history isn't in either list (it's hidden). **Practice and preview catches never reach the Log**, and say so. **Settings › Help › Replay the intro** (`openIntroPreview`) runs it as a preview on a throwaway copy of their settings: no prompts, refreshes or paywall, the offer always shows, nothing is saved, and a close button sits on every page. Tests: `test/ui/onboarding_test.dart`, `test/domain/first_look_test.dart`.
- **Catches live in the user's Photos, in a "LookUp" album** (`services/photo_library.dart`, `photo_manager`; Android: Pictures/LookUp, no permission). The Log keeps each picture's Photos id (`Sighting.photoAsset`) and only a small thumbnail, and shows it from Photos (`SightingPicture`); if Photos can't give it back (access refused, picture deleted, an id that didn't read back right after saving), the Log keeps or shows LookUp's own copy and the catch still counts. **We recommend Limited access**: before iOS's prompt, `PhotosAccessSheet` says so ("Limit Access…", Recommended; "you don't need to pick any photos"; then only "Don't Allow": Full Access gives them nothing, so it isn't listed), as do `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` and Settings › Photos (`photosAccessLine`). LookUp only ever reads its own album; never ask for Full access or read the rest of the library. Each picture carries its words in the file (`domain/photo/catch_tag.dart`: XMP with the sighting id, what, when, where) and its date and place on the Photos item. Tests: `test/ui/keep_catch_test.dart`, `test/domain/catch_tag_test.dart`; the Limited-access assumption is checked on a phone (`docs/mac-checklist.md`).
- **New phone:** catches come back from the LookUp album (`restoreCatches` and `BringBackCatches` in `ui/keep_catch.dart`, rule in `domain/log/restore.dart`): every picture carries its sighting in the file (`CatchTag`: id, what, when, where, kind, height, brightness), so badges come back too. Offered on an empty Log and in Settings › Photos. Android also has Auto Backup (on by default).
- **Help and legal** (`ui/help.dart`; pages in `relay/src/pages.ts`): Terms of Use (Apple's standard licence) and Privacy Policy links under anything that sells LookUp+ (`LegalLinks`: paywall and onboarding offer, which also gives the store's yearly price and that it renews); Settings ends with Help (Report a problem to hello@brilliantdisruptions.com with only the app version, the system and whether they have LookUp+). Keep `appVersion` in step with pubspec (a test checks) and the policy in step with what leaves the phone.
- **Rating prompt** (`domain/log/review_ask.dart`, `ui/review_prompt.dart`): only after the caught-it screen closes, never for a preview; from the 3rd catch and 3 days in, then every 4 months at most, 3 times ever.
- **Your week ahead** (`domain/scheduling/week_ahead.dart`): one note Sunday 5:40 PM local, rescheduled each refresh; only kinds they have on and their plan alerts for, never LookUp+; a quiet week says when the next thing is; nothing coming, no note. Off in Settings › Alerts.
- **The first badge ("Looked up") is earned by finishing onboarding** (`LookUpSettings.onboardedAt`) and shown once on Home (`first_badge_sheet.dart`). A catch never re-announces it.
- **Badges are mission patches** (`domain/log/sky_log.dart`, painted by `ui/badges/patch.dart`, board and sheet in `ui/badges/badge_board.dart`): each has its own picture (`BadgeArt`); levels go bronze, silver, gold (Station spotter 1/5/25, Regular 10/25/50); collections fill slots (`planetSlots`, `moonSlots`; each night's Moon is its own catch, id `obj:moon:YYYYMMDD`); seasonal patches are one per meteor shower per year (`seasonPatch`, `comingSeason`); secrets show only a riddle (and no hint) until earned. Locked patches show their outline, progress and the next real chance (`domain/log/next_chance.dart`), which never sends anything of its own. The caught-it screen spins the patch in and stitches it (`PatchArrives`), and says "Up a level" for a new tier (`CatchMoment.newBadges` uses `lastStepAt`). Only catches earn patches (never "missed it" answers or practice catches), they're all free, and their words never use degrees. Tests: `test/domain/badges_test.dart`, `test/ui/badge_board_test.dart`.
- **The Pro camera is LookUp+** (`PlusFeature.camera`; `ui/pro_camera_screen.dart`, entry from "Photograph it" on the Look and object sheets and the sky camera's Pro button). Pip's quick catch stays free: it's how anyone fills the Log. Plans per subject in `domain/photo/shot_plan.dart` (Moon: zoom lens, Looney-11 shutter, sharpest of 40; planets; stars by the 300 rule; trails, meteors and aurora), stacks in `frame_stack.dart`, the live exposure check and meteor spotting in `frame_check.dart`. Phone lenses have a fixed aperture: LookUp chooses the lens and says so. Never offer it for a solar eclipse (`subjectForEvent` returns null). Passes go to Catch it, now on for LookUp+ in the app; "Photograph it" on alerts stays off (`catchItOnAlerts`) until it's proven on phones.
- **Timelapses start and stop by themselves** (`domain/photo/timelapse_plan.dart`, `services/capture/timelapse_runner.dart`): from when it's 8° up or the event starts, to when it sinks, the night ends or it's stopped; one frame per interval so the clip runs about 15 s. Video comes from the native `lookup/timelapse` encoder (Android compiled by CI; iPhone's `LookUpTimelapse.swift` waits for a Mac, see the checklist); every timelapse also makes a trail picture, which is what an iPhone saves until then. Android's `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (for saving to the gallery) keeps `tools:replace` on its `maxSdkVersion`, or the manifest merge with the camera plugin fails.
